# Demo video

The 30-second demo of Armstrong Maps, published on 2026-11-15:
**https://youtu.be/xQKNnt-h_sA** (public, 29.8 s, 1080p).

Space Apps rules for the video: at most 30 seconds (a longer video makes the project ineligible
for global judging), and the link must be public.

## The talk

1. "Mars. Magnificent, and merciless. Before a crew steps outside, one question must be answered:
   will they come home?"
2. "Armstrong Maps answers it. Drawing on NASA's Mars Trek and the Curiosity Rover Location Map,
   it layers the terrain, checks every route, and guides the astronaut through the visor."
3. "This is our way to pursue the next frontier."

The captions are burned into the picture.

## The scenes, in order

| Scene | On screen | Kind |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | NASA mission footage, cut short, with the title | Real NASA video |
| Globe | The app's globe turning, choosing a site | Recording of the live app |
| Layers | The surface map with NASA layers switched on | Recording of the live app |
| Route | A walk along Curiosity's track: four waypoints, each with its task typed in, and the verdict | Recording of the live app |
| Surface | An astronaut walking before Mount Sharp | Generated with AI from the NASA mosaic PIA16101, marked on screen |
| Visor | The helmet display over the Gediz Vallis photograph (PIA26019) | Recording of the live app |
| Team | A photograph of the team at work | Real photograph |
| Final take | An astronaut walking back to the base, with the last line | Generated with AI from PIA26019, marked on screen |

The app scenes were recorded in real time from https://www.armstrongmaps.us.

## Sound and credits

- Ambient sound and effects: cut only from NASA recordings (the Mars 2020 launch broadcast, the
  Perseverance landing, InSight, the Perseverance microphones). No stock effects.
- Narration: a catalogue voice of ElevenLabs. No real person's voice was cloned.
- Music: original, generated with ElevenLabs.
- No NASA logo is in frame, and the video says "NASA data", never that the project is NASA's.

How AI was used in the video is declared on the project page and in the README.
