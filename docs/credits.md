# Credits and licences

Every dataset, picture, sound, typeface and library the app uses, with who made it and the terms
it comes under. The same credits are on screen where each one is shown, and in the app at
`/docs` (Data and APIs, and Software and credits). The address of every feed is in
`src/data/sources.ts`.

Where a source states no licence, the table says so and the app credits it by name. It is not
read as permission for anything beyond showing the data with its credit.

## NASA data

Material made by NASA is generally not subject to copyright in the United States; the terms are
NASA's [media usage guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). They
do not allow the NASA logotype or insignia to be used, and the app does not use them. Material
made with partners carries their names in its credit.

### Sound

Wind recorded by the SuperCam microphone of Perseverance at Jezero, sol 4 (22 February 2021):
[Perseverance Rover's SuperCam Records Wind on Mars](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/perseverance-rovers-supercam-records-wind-on-mars/),
NASA/JPL-Caltech/LANL/CNES/CNRS/ISAE-Supaero. The file in `public/audio` is the published MP3,
unchanged; the app plays it louder through a limiter (see [assumptions.md](assumptions.md)). The
alert and message tones are generated by the app.
